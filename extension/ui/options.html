Built for switching sites

Your place is saved here.

The popup shows the current title, season, episode, and playback position. Your library brings together episodes from every website.

When a site hides the title

Use “Set title / episode” in the popup. MAL matches are remembered by title and season, across sites.

Different episode numbering?

Use the episode offset when linking a MAL season. A site’s episode 25 can become episode 1 on MAL with an offset of −24. Split cours may also need their own match.

Continue where you left off

If you open a different episode, a prompt offers your saved episode. On the same episode, Continue resumes your saved timestamp, including in embedded players. Choose Stay here to keep watching as-is.

Privacy, plainly.

History and login tokens live in this browser’s extension storage. When connected, anime title searches, MAL IDs, and watched episode counts go directly to MyAnimeList. Page URLs and video files are never uploaded. No analytics, proxy, or external server.

Site access is needed to read episode metadata and playback, including embedded players. Browser system pages, restricted domains, closed shadow players, and sites with no exposed metadata may need a correction.

Disconnect removes saved login tokens. Local history stays available. Export excludes tokens and client settings.

Custom site rules

Optional CSS selectors for sites whose metadata needs help. No script execution. Episode and season selectors can return either a number or a label such as “Episode 4”.

Example: [{"hostname":"example.com","titleSelector":"h1","episodeSelector":".current-episode","seasonSelector":".season"}]
